KIN

KIN Global is Singapore's largest sports events management company, publicly listed on the SGX. The Group is responsible for landmark events including the World Aquatics Championships, FISE Singapore, HSBC SVNS, the FIDE World Championship and WTT Singapore Smash. We were engaged to design and build a new corporate website from the ground up. The brief was unusually layered: the site had to satisfy institutional readers — investors, regulators, partners — with the credibility a listed company requires, while also conveying the spectacle, scale and emotional weight of the events KIN actually delivers. Two audiences, one platform, no compromises. The solution is a black-canvas, image-led system organised around three propositions — Sports, Experiential, Cultural — and three audiences — investors, prospective clients and the broader public. A portfolio of immersive case studies, an SGX-integrated investor-relations module, executive narrative pages and a long-form insights stream all share a single coherent visual language.
