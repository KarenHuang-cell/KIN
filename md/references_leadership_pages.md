## Patterns to watch for (the long-mobile-list problem)

When browsing the references above on your phone, look for these treatments:

1. **Photo + name only on mobile; bio expands on tap.** Cuts visible length ~70%.
2. **Filter chips at top** (Board / Executive / Operating) — lets users jump to a subgroup.
3. **Hero exec first, then grid of others.** Establishes hierarchy without 8 equal-weight cards.
4. **Cards collapse to photo + name on mobile, full bio reveals on tap or hover on desktop.**
