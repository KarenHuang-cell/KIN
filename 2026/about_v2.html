About Us

From stadium floors to museum halls

KIN was founded on a simple conviction: world-class events and cultural experiences can transform a city’s identity, economy, and global standing. We obsess over every detail — from the first planning meeting to the final celebration — because that’s what turns a good event into a defining one. Today, we’re a leading event tourism company — driving visitor engagement and destination growth through three specialised divisions: sports and live events, experiential design and build, and cultural and IP programming.

Built on sport,
expanding the stage

As one of Singapore’s leading event management companies, KIN earned its reputation delivering international sporting championships — events watched by millions that turned host cities into global destinations. That same operational DNA now powers immersive exhibitions at national museums, large-scale cultural festivals, touring IP experiences, and destination attractions built from the ground up.

We work with international federations, tourism boards, and cultural institutions to plan, design, and deliver experiences that drive visitor arrivals, generate economic impact, and earn international recognition. From event planning and venue transformation to experiential design and cultural programming — we organise it all, end to end.
