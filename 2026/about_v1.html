01 — Origin

From stadium floors to museum halls.

World-class events and cultural experiences can transform a city's identity, economy, and global standing — and we obsess over every detail that makes that real.

KIN was founded on a simple conviction: a great event isn't just a calendar entry — it's a defining moment for a city. Today we're a leading event tourism company, driving visitor engagement and destination growth through three specialised divisions: sports and live events, experiential design and build, and cultural and IP programming.

As one of Singapore's leading event management companies, KIN earned its reputation delivering international sporting championships — events watched by millions that turned host cities into global destinations. That same operational DNA now powers immersive exhibitions at national museums, large-scale cultural festivals, touring IP experiences, and destination attractions built from the ground up.
