Summary My take, ranked
FOR KIN, IN ORDER OF FIT

1. Panel A — Plus Jakarta only. If we trust the brand, this is the most modern, most sport-native, lowest risk. The 200-weight "is" inside an 800-weight line gives all the contrast we need. No second family to maintain.

2. Panel D — Roboto Slab. If we want a second face, this is the sportiest. Reads as signage, jersey numbers, broadcast graphics. The cultural side gets carried by Plus Jakarta in the body.

3. Panel E — Petrona. Chunky transitional with shoulders. Less common than the rest, gives genuine character without prettiness. Worth a real look.

4. Panel F — Roboto Serif. The safe pick. Quietly elegant. Risk of generic.

Reject: Newsreader and Fraunces — both too pretty for the brief, as you flagged.
