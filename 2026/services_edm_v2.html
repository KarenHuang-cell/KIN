02 — The work

End-to-end events delivery.

We specialise in world-class international sporting events that attract global audiences, generate tourism dollars, and elevate destination profiles.

Founded in 2017, KIN is the largest sports events management company in Singapore. We operate as principal commercial actors for sports events — contracting directly with rights holders or local organising committees to deliver full-scale events management services, from conceptualisation and planning through to execution.
