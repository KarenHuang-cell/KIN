Accreditations

At KIN Global, we hold ourselves to the highest standards across quality, safety, sustainability, and workforce development. Our accreditations and certifications reflect our commitment to delivering world-class event tourism experiences — every project, every venue, every team.

ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management

International standard for quality management systems, recognising frameworks for consistently meeting customer and regulatory requirements.

ISO 14001:2015 Environmental Management

Environmental management systems standard, demonstrating practices for identifying and reducing environmental impact across operations.

ISO 45001:2018 Occupational Health & Safety

Occupational health and safety management standard, affirming practices for protecting workers and visitors on every site.

bizSAFE STAR

The highest tier of Singapore's Workplace Safety and Health Council bizSAFE programme, awarded to organisations with mature workplace safety and health management systems.

Building & Construction Authority

Singapore's national regulator for the built environment, confirming registered status for construction and built-environment works.

Enterprise 50 (E50) Awards

Annual award recognising Singapore's top 50 privately-held companies for innovation, sustainable growth, and contribution to the local economy.

GeBIZ EPPU

Registered Government Supplier on Singapore's GeBIZ procurement system, indicating eligibility to participate in public sector tenders.

Progressive Wage Mark

Singapore Ministry of Manpower mark recognising employers who pay all eligible workers progressive wages, affirming fair and equitable compensation practices.
